Set up your SuperWho app without an App Store download.

It's simple and gets you going in just 90 seconds. You'll also have a SuperWho app icon on your phone for easy access to your SuperWho page or dashboard.

You'll hear from your team the moment something's ready.

No checking email, no hunting for links. Your SuperWho icon sits right on your Home Screen and pings your phone the second your team posts an update, reply, or draft to approve.

iPhone & iPad · ~90 seconds

3 quick steps in Safari

  1. Tap Share □↑ at the bottom of Safari

    If your page opened inside Mail or Messages, tap the Safari compass first.

  2. Tap Add to Home Screen → Add

    This puts your SuperWho app icon right on your Home Screen — no App Store needed.

  3. Open SuperWho from your Home Screen & tap Allow

    Tap the new icon, tap Turn on notifications, then tap Allow.

Android · ~30 seconds

2 quick steps on Android

  1. Tap Turn on notifications → Allow

    You'll see this button on your welcome screen and in Messages. Your phone alerts are live right away.

  2. Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen

    Adds the SuperWho app icon to your Home Screen — no Play Store download needed.

Mac & Windows · ~15 seconds

1 click on your computer

  1. Click Turn on notifications → Allow

    Updates pop up on your screen whenever your browser is open.

  2. Do the 90-second setup on your phone too

    Open your same page link on your iPhone or Android phone so alerts reach you anywhere.

Quick fixes

If something doesn't pop up

I don't see "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone

Open your link in Safari (tap the bottom-right compass icon if you're viewing it inside Mail or Messages).

I tapped "Don't Allow" earlier

On iPhone: Settings → Notifications → SuperWho → Allow Notifications. On Android: tap the icon next to the web address → Permissions → Notifications → Allow.
